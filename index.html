Only 15 students are taken in one batch so that every student gets opportunities to participate and practise speaking.

No. The focus is on using English confidently. Grammar is taught where necessary, but the learning is designed around speaking and practical communication.

Absolutely. The course is designed to help learners gradually overcome hesitation and become more comfortable speaking English.
